about_me

Currently pursuing a dual study degree in IT Security at the Baden-Wuerttemberg Cooperative State University (DHBW) Ravensburg, Campus Friedrichshafen, I am building a strong foundation in cybersecurity while gaining hands-on experience in systems engineering.

During my studies, I focus on microkernel architectures (such as L4Re) and real-time communication middleware (DDS, MQTT). I am particularly interested in how secure software is built from the ground up — from memory-safe implementations to well-designed protocol stacks.

Whether analyzing network topologies using Containerlab or working on C/Rust projects for embedded systems, I continuously expand my skills with the goal of building robust, secure, and resilient systems.
